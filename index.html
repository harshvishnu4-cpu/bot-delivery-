Mission info

Help Zippy take hot biryani to Mrs. Sharma's house. Zippy only drives straight, so you design its rules.

How rules work
  • IFchecks what Zippy sees on the road.
  • THENis what Zippy does when it is true.
  • ELSEis what Zippy does when it is not true.
  • ANDboth conditions must be true.
  • ORone or both conditions is enough.
  • 1 · 2 · 3rules are checked top to bottom. The first true rule wins.
How to play
  • Dragor tap a tile to put it in a space.
  • Runto test your rules on the road.
  • Hintthe bulb button gives a clue when you're stuck.
